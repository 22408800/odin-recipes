stew with spaghetti and egg

Ingredients:

  • peppper base: 6 large plum tomatoes, 2 red bell peppers (tatashe), 1 large onion, 1-2 scotch bonnet peppers (ata rodo, to taste).
  • stew base: 1/2 cup vegetable oil, 70g tomato paste( 1 samll can), 1 medium onions(sliced).
  • protein & stock: 1 lb beef, chcken or goat meat, reserved meat stock(about 1-2 cups)
  • seasoning: 2 bouillon cubes (Knorr or maggi), 1tsp curry powder, 1 tsp dried thyme, 2 bay leaves, salt to taste
