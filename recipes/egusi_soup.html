Egusi Soup

Ingredients:

  • Egusi base: 2 cups ground egusi seeds (melon seeds), 1/2 sup warm water (or stock).
  • Pepper blend: 2 red bell peppers (tatashe), 1 large onion, 1-2 scotch bonnet peppers (ata rodo, to taste).
  • Proteins: 1.5 lbs mixed meat (goat meat, beef, or cow skin/kpomo), 1 piece stockfish (soaked), 1 smoked piece stockfish (soaked), i smoked fish (cleaned and deboned).
  • Soup base & oil: 1/2 cup red palm oil, 2 cups meat stock, 2 tbsp ground crayfish.
  • Seasoning: 2 bouillon cubes, 2 tbsp iru (fermented locust beans, optional), salt to taste.
  • Greens: 2 cups chopped spinach, ugu (fluted pumpkin leaves), or bitterleaf.

Steps

  1. Cook the Meats & Stockfish:

    Builds deep savory stock

    Boil the beef, goat meat, and pre-soaked stockfish in a pot with half a sliced onion, 1 bouillon cube, an salt until tender. Reserve 2 cups of the meat stock and set aside.

  2. Prepare the Egusi Paste:

    Prevents clumping later

    Mix the ground egusi seeds with warm water or stock in a small bowl to form a thick paste. Set aside Blend the bell peppers, scotch bonnets, and remaining onion until coarsely smooth.

  3. Fry & Form Lumps:

    Creates distinctive lump texture

    Heat palm oil in apot over medium heat. Add the egusi paste ina small spoonfuls into the hot oil. Do not stir immediately; let it fry on low heat for 5-7 minutes so it forms a signature egusi lumps, then gently turn.

  4. Add Pepper Blend & Proteins:

    Melds aromatics and proteins

    Pour in the blended pepper mixture, ground crayfish, iru (locust beans), and remaining bouillon cube. Cook for 10 minutes until the oil starts to float to the top. Add the cooked meats, stockfish, smoked fish, and reserved stock.

  5. Add Greens & Simmer:

    Preserves color and nutrients

    Stir gently and let the soup simmer for 10 minutes on low heat. Add the chooped spinach or ugu leaves,stir, and cook for and additional 3-5 minutes until the grens wilt. Taste and adjust salt.

    Serve hot alongside swallows like pounded yam, eba (garri), fufu or amala.
